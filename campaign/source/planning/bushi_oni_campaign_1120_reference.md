# Part I: The State of the Empire (End of 1119)

## The Throne and Imperial Court

**Emperor:** Hantei XXXVIII (Jodan) sits upon the throne. He has two sons:
- **Hantei Sotorii** – the elder, hot-tempered and difficult
- **Hantei Daisetsu** – the younger, scholarly and thoughtful

**Empress:** Hantei Hochiahime (née Crane)

**Emerald Champion:** Doji Satsume (also Crane Clan Champion) [dies in 1123]

**Imperial Herald:** Miya Satoshi

There is no Imperial Regent or Imperial Advisor at this time. The Emperor rules directly.

## Recent History (What Everyone Knows)

### The Crane-Lion Conflict
- **~60 years ago:** The Lion captured Toshi Ranbo and the Osari Plains from the Crane
- **~1103:** The Crane captured the Kintani Valley
- The border remains tense; skirmishes are common

### The Dragon's Isolation
- The Dragon Clan remains largely withdrawn in their mountains
- Togashi Yokuni rarely appears in public
- The clan's motives are inscrutable to outsiders

### The Crab's Burden
- The Crab Clan maintains the Kaiu Wall against the Shadowlands
- Resources are always stretched thin
- Hida Kisada grows frustrated with the Empire's lack of support

### The Perfect Land Sect (1118)
- A heretical Buddhist sect was scattered by Imperial forces
- Survivors have gone underground
- The sect promises escape from the Celestial Wheel's suffering

### Minor Clan Tensions
- **Yoritomo** leads the Mantis, growing increasingly bold and ambitious
- The Mantis are not yet recognized as a Great Clan
- Piracy in the seas is increasing

## Established Relationships and Tensions

### Romantic/Political
- **Akodo Arasou & Matsu Tsuko** – Betrothed; passionate relationship
- **Bayushi Shoju & Bayushi Kachiko** – Married; Kachiko is ambitious
- **Doji Hotaru** – Unmarried; the question of her marriage is politically significant

### Rivalries
- **Akodo Arasou vs. Doji Satsume** – Personal enmity over Toshi Ranbo
- **Hida Kisada vs. the Imperial Court** – Kisada believes the Empire ignores the Shadowlands threat

### Hidden Threats (Unknown to Most)
- **Kuni Yori** is conducting experiments with Shadowlands corruption

- The **Kolat** conspiracy operates in the shadows (Miya Satoshi is secretly a member)

# Part II: Events of Early 1120 (January–April)

These are the canonical events you may wish to modify or build upon.

## Confirmed 1120 Events

### The Tsunami (Reference: "Her Father's Daughter")
- **What:** A massive tsunami strikes the coastline, devastating Mantis holdings
- **When:** Early 1120 (approximately 3 years before the main storyline)
- **Significance:** This disaster shapes Yoritomo's ambitions and the Mantis Clan's desperation

### The Heart of the Mountain (Reference: "The Heart of the Mountain")
- **What:** Yoritomo steals a powerful nemuranai called "The Heart of the Mountain"
- **When:** Shortly before or during the Tsunami
- **Connection:** The theft may be connected to the Tsunami itself
- **Significance:** Establishes Yoritomo as willing to take dramatic action

### The Assassination at Kyotei Castle (Reference: "Wedding at Kyotei Castle" RPG)
- **What:** Tsume Retsu, lord of Kyotei Castle in the Kintani Valley, is assassinated
- **When:** 1120
- **Context:** Retsu had ruled for 17 years after capturing both Kyotei Castle and the Kintani Valley
- **Significance:** Creates a power vacuum in a contested border region between Crane and Lion

---

## What Is NOT Yet Happening

The following events are still **3 years away** and should not be foreshadowed too heavily:

- Doji Satsume is still alive and serving as both Clan Champion and Emerald Champion
- Shiba Ujimitsu is still the Phoenix Champion
- Akodo Arasou is still alive
- The Emperor is healthy
- The Unicorn-Lion war has not begun
- Toshi Ranbo is still held by the Crane

# Part III: Major Plot Threads (1120–1124)

Below are the major storylines that will develop over the next four years, with notes on their status as of early 1120.

---

## Plot 1: The Succession Crisis

**Summary:** Emperor Hantei XXXVIII's two sons—the difficult Sotorii and the capable Daisetsu—represent a brewing succession crisis that will eventually tear the Empire apart.

**Status in Early 1120:** 
- **Dormant** – Sotorii is still a child (his gempukku is in March 1123)
- The brothers' rivalry is present but not yet critical
- No formal heir has been named

**Key Figures:**
- Hantei XXXVIII (Emperor)
- Hantei Sotorii (elder son)
- Hantei Daisetsu (younger son)
- Bayushi Kachiko (will become Imperial Advisor)
- Kakita Yoshi (will become Regent)

**Where It's Going:**
- Sotorii becomes increasingly unstable
- Kachiko manipulates events to favor Daisetsu
- Culminates in the Emperor's death (Sept 1123) and civil war

**Campaign Use:** Plant seeds of the brothers' personalities. Sotorii's temper, Daisetsu's wisdom. NPCs might gossip about which son would make a better Emperor.

---

## Plot 2: The Crane-Lion War

**Summary:** Generations of conflict over the Osari Plains and Toshi Ranbo will erupt into open warfare, claiming the lives of both clan champions.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Simmering** – Toshi Ranbo is held by the Crane (captured ~1121)
- Tsume Retsu's assassination creates new instability
- Akodo Arasou burns with desire to retake lost territory

**Key Figures:**
- Akodo Arasou (Lion Champion) – dies May 1123
- Doji Satsume (Crane Champion) – dies April 1123
- Doji Hotaru (becomes Crane Champion ~1121-1122)
- Matsu Tsuko (Arasou's lover, will become Champion)
- Doji Kuwanan (Satsume's son)
- Daidoji Uji (Crane general)

**Key Locations:**
- Toshi Ranbo (Violence Behind Courtliness City)
- Osari Plains
- Kyuden Kakita
- Kintani Valley / Kyotei Castle

**Where It's Going:**
- Arasou dies attacking Toshi Ranbo (May 1123)
- Lion eventually capture Kyuden Kakita (Sept 1123)
- Crane retake it briefly before losing it again
- War only ends when larger threats emerge

**Campaign Use:** The Tsume Retsu assassination is happening NOW. PCs could investigate, be caught in the fallout, or have connections to the contested territories.

---

## Plot 3: The Unicorn-Lion War

**Summary:** A separate conflict between Lion and Unicorn erupts over old grievances, drawing both clans' attention away from other threats.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Distant** – Won't formally begin until April 1123
- Historical grievances exist (Utaku Kamoko's mother was killed by Lion ~1112)
- No active hostilities

**Key Figures:**
- Shinjo Altansarnai (Unicorn Champion)
- Utaku Kamoko (driven by personal vendetta)
- Ide Tadaji (diplomat trying to prevent war)
- Shinjo Shono (Altansarnai's son)
- Various Lion generals

**Key Locations:**
- Hisu Mori Mura (Unicorn village, repeatedly contested)
- The northern plains

**Where It's Going:**
- Unicorn declare war April 1123
- Back-and-forth battles over Hisu Mori Mura
- Eventually both sides join alliance against greater threat

**Campaign Use:** The old wound of Kamoko's mother could be explored. Unicorn-Lion tensions simmer beneath diplomatic surfaces.

---

## Plot 4: The Shadowlands Rising

**Summary:** Kuni Yori's dark experiments and the weakening of the Kaiu Wall will lead to a massive Shadowlands incursion, culminating in the Battle of Cherry Blossom Snow.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Early Stage** – Kuni Yori is already conducting experiments
- The Wall holds, but resources are strained
- No major breaches yet

**Key Figures:**
- Hida Kisada (Crab Champion)
- Kuni Yori (Kuni Daimyo, secretly corrupt)
- Hida Sukune (Kisada's son)
- Hida O-Ushi (Kisada's daughter)
- Yasuki Oguri (will investigate the Wall's fall)
- Isawa Tadaka (will become involved with dark magic)

**Key Locations:**
- The Kaiu Wall
- Shiro Kuni
- Various watchtowers (especially the 9th)
- Hiruma Castle

**Where It's Going:**
- Kuni Yori's corruption spreads
- Ishigaki Watchtower falls (Aug 1123)
- The Wall itself falls (Dec 1123)
- Massive oni invasion
- Battle of Cherry Blossom Snow (Jan 1124)

**Campaign Use:** Crab PCs might notice something wrong with Kuni lands. Shadowlands activity could be increasing. Yori's experiments might need "subjects."

---

## Plot 5: The Scorpion Coup

**Summary:** Bayushi Shoju, believing he is saving the Empire from a prophesied doom, will kill the Emperor and seize power—only to be defeated and driven to open a Black Scroll.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Seeds Being Planted** – Shoju possesses knowledge of dark prophecies
- Kachiko is positioning herself at court
- The Scorpion are patient

**Key Figures:**
- Bayushi Shoju (Champion)
- Bayushi Kachiko (his wife, Imperial Advisor by 1123)
- Bayushi Yojiro (will become Champion after Shoju)
- Bayushi Aramoro (Shoju's brother, assassin)
- Yogo Junzo (Yogo Daimyo, will die defending a cursed sword)

**Key Locations:**
- Kyuden Bayushi
- Otosan Uchi (Imperial Capital)
- Toshi Ranbo (given to Scorpion in 1123)
- Castle of Learning (Yogo stronghold)

**Where It's Going:**
- Shoju kills Emperor (Sept 1123)
- Brief Scorpion regency
- Shoju deposed, opens Black Scroll
- Second coup attempt (Dec 1123)
- Shoju's final defeat (Jan 1124)

**Campaign Use:** Scorpion machinations are always present. Kachiko is building her network. Whispers of ancient prophecies might surface.

---

## Plot 6: The Dragon's Prophecy

**Summary:** Togashi Yokuni has foreseen coming catastrophe and is quietly positioning his clan—and specific individuals—to play crucial roles.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Ongoing** – Yokuni always sees further than others
- Togashi Mitsu is searching for someone named Senzai
- The Dragon remain mysterious

**Key Figures:**
- Togashi Yokuni (Champion, actually the Kami Togashi)
- Togashi Mitsu (will become Champion after Yokuni)
- Mirumoto Hitomi (will kill Shoju, driven by vengeance)
- Agasha Sumiko (Ruby Champion, truth-seeker)
- Kitsuki Chiari (investigator who finds crucial evidence)

**Key Locations:**
- Kyuden Togashi
- Various Dragon monasteries

**Where It's Going:**
- Yokuni sends agents to key locations
- Hitomi develops obsession with revenge
- Mitsu gathers the "army of the rising wave"
- Yokuni sacrifices himself at Cherry Blossom Snow

**Campaign Use:** Dragon NPCs might appear with cryptic warnings. Yokuni could take interest in PCs. Prophecy fragments might surface.

---

## Plot 7: The Phoenix Schism

**Summary:** The Phoenix Clan struggles with questions of forbidden magic, the role of the Elemental Masters, and internal conflicts that will cost them their champion and one of their greatest shugenja.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Early Stage** – The Council functions, but tensions exist
- Isawa Tadaka is young and ambitious
- Isawa Ujina (Master of Void) is stable

**Key Figures:**
- Shiba Ujimitsu (Champion) – dies April 1123
- Shiba Tsukune (successor)
- Isawa Tadaka (becomes Master of Earth, eventually corrupted)
- Isawa Ujina (Master of Void) – disappears July 1123
- Isawa Kaede (will marry Toturi)
- Asako Tsuki (investigator, discovers Yori's corruption)

**Key Locations:**
- Kyuden Isawa
- Kaito lands
- The Shrine of Azusa

**Where It's Going:**
- Ujimitsu dies, Tsukune becomes Champion
- Tadaka takes forbidden path to gain power against Shadowlands
- Ujina vanishes mysteriously
- Tadaka ultimately redeems himself at Cherry Blossom Snow

**Campaign Use:** Phoenix internal politics are complex. Questions of how far one can go to fight evil. Kaito lands have spiritual significance.

---

## Plot 8: The Perfect Land Sect

**Summary:** A heretical religious movement promises escape from suffering, attracting desperate peasants and creating a shadow network that will shelter fleeing Imperial heirs.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Recently Scattered** – The sect was broken up in 1118
- Survivors are regrouping in secret
- The movement continues to attract followers

**Key Figures:**
- Various sect leaders (unnamed)
- Togashi Mitsu (will infiltrate them)
- Yuki (undercover Scorpion agent)
- Eventually shelters Daisetsu and his protectors

**Key Locations:**
- Remote villages
- Mountain hideaways
- Among the peasant class everywhere

**Where It's Going:**
- Sect rebuilds in secret
- Scorpion agents infiltrate
- Becomes refuge for fleeing princes
- Mitsu spends winter 1123-1124 with them

**Campaign Use:** PCs might encounter the sect, investigate them, or have family members who joined. Questions of faith and suffering.

---

## Plot 9: The Mantis Ambition

**Summary:** Yoritomo seeks Great Clan status for the Mantis through any means necessary, including theft, piracy, and leveraging disasters.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Active** – The Tsunami is happening NOW
- Yoritomo has stolen the Heart of the Mountain
- The Mantis are desperate and dangerous

**Key Figures:**
- Yoritomo (Mantis Champion)
- Yoritomo Linmei (his wife)
- Various Mantis captains

**Key Locations:**
- "Kyuden" Gotei (Mantis headquarters)
- The coastal waters
- Islands of the Mantis

**Where It's Going:**
- Mantis leverage the chaos of 1123
- Kidnap Yasuki Taka for leverage
- Eventually gain Great Clan status

**Campaign Use:** The Tsunami is immediate. Mantis desperation could drive many plot hooks. Yoritomo is a wildcard.

---

## Plot 10: The Kolat Conspiracy

**Summary:** An ancient conspiracy against the Kami and the Celestial Order operates in the shadows, with agents in every clan.

**Status in Early 1120:**
- **Always Active** – The Kolat have existed for centuries
- Miya Satoshi (Imperial Herald) is a member
- They watch, wait, and manipulate

**Key Figures:**
- Miya Satoshi (Imperial Herald, secret Kolat)
- Various unnamed masters
- Agents in all clans

**Where It's Going:**
- Kolat observe the chaos
- May try to exploit the civil war
- Ultimately a background threat

**Campaign Use:** Any NPC could secretly be Kolat. They oppose divine authority itself. Excellent long-term conspiracy.
