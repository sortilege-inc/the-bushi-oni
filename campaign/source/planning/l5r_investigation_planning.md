# Bushy Oni Investigation - Session Planning

## Current Investigation State

### What the Players Have Established

| Evidence | Implication |
|----------|-------------|
| Deaths accelerating (months → weeks → days) | Something is escalating or feeding |
| Ujiaki's elongated arm, worn jade, mental lapses | Taint exposure, NOT illusion |
| Toshiyasu's body: bisected, claw marks, pustules at spine | Beast/Oni attack with taint corruption |
| Defensive wounds from own wakizashi (blade facing him) | Disarmed or weapon twisted mid-fight |
| Toshiyasu's last tea ceremony was with Bayushi Aramoro | Scorpion connection to final hours |
| Divination: Ujiaki fleeing to Lion lands, seeking healer he trusts | He knows he's tainted, doesn't feel safe here |

### Open Questions They Haven't Answered

- How is something getting into secured rooms?
- What connects the victims?
- Who benefits?
- Why is Ujiaki lying about "illusion"?

---

## Proposed Scene Structure

### Scene 1: Asako Kikue Interview

**Purpose:** Follow up on tea ceremony lead, get Phoenix/official investigation perspective

She's useful because she wears two hats: tea ceremony attendee AND original investigative team member. She can:

- Confirm Toshiyasu seemed troubled in his final days
- Share what the official investigation concluded (and where it hit walls)
- Mention that Aramoro's tea ceremony was unusual—Toshiyasu requested it, not Aramoro
- Hint that political pressure shut down certain lines of inquiry
- If pressed: admit the Phoenix contingent found taint traces but were told to keep it quiet

**Potential tension:** She's caught between wanting truth and institutional loyalty. Players can push her toward disclosure or alienate her.

---

### Scene 2: News of Ujiaki's Death

**Purpose:** Raise stakes, eliminate witness, create urgency

They hear it secondhand—a bridge collapsed on the road to Lion lands. Ujiaki's caravan went down. No survivors.

This lands harder because:

- They just SAW him yesterday
- They know he was fleeing to get help
- The divination showed his future... which is now cut short

Let them sit with it a moment. Then someone (Taigan? A messenger?) notes: "That bridge has stood for forty years."

---

### Scene 3: Bridge Collapse Investigation (Optional but Recommended)

**Purpose:** Confirm sabotage, show the conspiracy has reach

If they go to the site:

- **Engineering/Craft roll:** Support beams were weakened deliberately. Cut marks hidden by the break.
- **Theology/Medicine:** Ujiaki's body (if recoverable) shows the taint had progressed significantly—he might not have survived the treatment anyway
- **Theology/Commune:** Spirits in the area are disturbed, frightened—something unnatural passed through recently
- **Investigation:** Caravan guards were specifically reassigned last minute. Someone knew the route.

This tells them: **whoever is behind this has institutional access and is cleaning up loose ends.**

---

### Scene 4: Toshiyasu's Room → Tunnel Discovery

**Purpose:** Answer "how is it getting in," open new mystery

The room itself might seem unremarkable at first. But:

- **Kitsuki's Method or careful investigation:** A section of floor has been disturbed. Dust patterns are wrong.
- **Below:** A hidden passage. Old—older than the current buildings. Part of an infrastructure nobody talks about.

**Questions this raises:**

- Who knows about these tunnels?
- Do they connect to other death sites?
- Are they natural, or constructed? By whom?

You can have them encounter evidence of recent use: claw marks on stone, a scrap of fabric, residual spiritual corruption.

---

### Scene 5: Other Rooms Investigation

**Purpose:** Confirm or complicate the tunnel theory

Two possibilities:

**Option A - Pattern confirmed:** Both other rooms connect to the tunnel system. The killer has been using the same method. This suggests planning and knowledge of the infrastructure.

**Option B - One room doesn't connect:** Miya Satoshi's room has no tunnel access. So how was he killed? Different method, different killer, or the tunnels aren't the only answer? This complicates things productively.

**Recommendation:** Lean toward Option B—it prevents the investigation from becoming too linear.

---

### Scene 6: Political Pressure

**Purpose:** Show conspiracy's reach, create moral tension

Someone warns them off. Options:

- **Didoji Masahiro:** "You've done admirable work. But some doors, once opened, cannot be closed. Consider whether the Empire is served by what you might find."
- **An Imperial functionary:** "The Emerald Champion's office appreciates your diligence. Your investigation is concluded. File your report."
- **An anonymous letter:** Names of people they care about. No threat stated. None needed.

This forces a choice: comply and stay safe, or push forward knowing there are consequences.

---

### Scene 7: Bayushi Aramoro

**Purpose:** Climactic interview, Scorpion ambiguity, possible alliance or threat

He doesn't avoid them—he *summons* them. Or appears where they're staying, unannounced, with sake.

Aramoro is dangerous because he's *helpful.* He can:

- Confirm Toshiyasu was investigating something and wanted Scorpion resources
- Suggest the cover-up comes from above—someone powerful wants this buried
- Offer assistance... for a price, or a future favor
- Imply he knows about the tunnels: "The palace has many secrets. Some older than the clans."

**The ambiguity:** Is he a suspect? An ally? Using them as pawns? All three?

Let him leave them with more questions than answers, but one concrete lead they didn't have before.

---

## Suggested Scene Order

1. **Kikue** — context, official investigation gaps
2. **Ujiaki's death** — stakes, urgency
3. **Bridge investigation** — sabotage confirmation
4. **Toshiyasu's room / tunnels** — mechanical revelation
5. **Other rooms** — complicate or confirm
6. **Political pressure** — moral stakes
7. **Aramoro** — climax, new direction

---

## Notes for GM

### Pacing Considerations

- Scenes 1-3 can happen in quick succession (same session)
- Scene 4 is a major revelation—give it room to breathe
- Scene 6 works best after they've committed to pushing forward
- Aramoro should feel like a reward for persistence

### Player Character Hooks

- **Hasumi (Kitsuki):** Forensic details, Kitsuki's Method opportunities in tunnels and bridge
- **Endo (Phoenix Shugenja):** Spiritual investigation, possible Phoenix clan complications via Kikue
- **Bayushi Taigan:** Scorpion politics, Aramoro interaction, potential loyalty conflicts
- **Rinji:** Give them a moment to shine—perhaps they notice something others miss

### Clue Redundancy

If players miss something critical, have it resurface through:

- Taigan's Scorpion contacts
- A second witness coming forward
- Physical evidence at a later scene
- An NPC directly stating what they should have found
