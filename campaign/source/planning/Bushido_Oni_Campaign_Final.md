# Bushido Oni Campaign — Final Revision

## Overview

A Legend of the Five Rings campaign set in early 1120, blending courtly intrigue, supernatural horror, wilderness survival, and political conspiracy. The campaign uses **troupe-style play**, allowing players to control multiple characters across different investigation threads that eventually converge.

The murders of the Emperor's most trusted advisors—informal confidants who embody the virtues of Bushidō—trigger a crisis that exposes three separate conspiracies operating in dangerous parallel.

---

## The Seven Imperial Advisors

The Emperor maintains seven trusted advisors—not formal positions, but individuals he confides in privately, each renowned as an exemplar of a specific virtue of Bushidō. In public, they are paragons of samurai virtue. In private, each harbors secrets that would shatter their carefully maintained reputations.

### THE DEAD

#### **Miya Satoshi** *(First Victim)*
**Clan:** Imperial (Miya Family) | **Role:** Daimyo, Imperial Herald
**Public Virtue:** **Makoto (Honesty/Sincerity)**

**Public Reputation:** For twenty years, Miya Satoshi served as the Emperor's Herald, carrying proclamations to every corner of the Empire. He was famous for his unwavering integrity—it was said that Satoshi had never told a lie in his life, that his word was as unchangeable as stone. His photographic memory and reputation for absolute honesty made his testimony irrefutable. People confessed their secrets around him, believing he would speak only truth.

**The Secret:** Satoshi's entire reputation was built on careful omission and technical truth. He mastered the art of answering questions that weren't asked, of speaking truths that implied falsehoods. But his deepest secret was far darker: Satoshi was a **Kolat agent**, serving an ancient conspiracy against the Celestial Order itself. Every "sincere" word he spoke served a hidden agenda. His position as Imperial Herald gave the Kolat unparalleled access to Imperial communications.

**Death:** Torn apart by an oni in his private chambers. The Crane conspiracy ordered his death as a distraction—they had no idea they had just assassinated a Kolat operative.

---

#### **Doji Toshiyasu** *(Second Victim)*
**Clan:** Crane (Doji Family) | **Role:** Tea Master
**Public Virtue:** **Rei (Respect/Courtesy)**

**Public Reputation:** Doji Toshiyasu was considered the most courteous samurai in Rokugan—a Tea Master whose ceremonies were legendary for their perfect grace. Every guest who attended his tea room felt honored, respected, valued. He could broker peace between bitter enemies through pure force of refined hospitality. His death was considered a catastrophic loss to Imperial culture.

**The Secret:** Toshiyasu's famous tea ceremonies were intelligence operations. Every guest who relaxed in his presence, every lord who confided over their cup, every lady who gossiped while admiring the flowers—he remembered everything. His "perfect courtesy" was a trap that made people comfortable enough to reveal their secrets.

More than that: Toshiyasu was an assassin of reputations. He killed with whispers, not blades. Carefully placed words destroyed careers, shattered alliances, drove men to fall upon their own swords in shame. Nothing could ever be traced back to his loose lips. He was courtesy weaponized.

**Death:** Torn apart by an oni, just like Satoshi. But this was no Crane operation—this was **Kolat revenge**. The conspiracy had discovered that Satoshi was theirs, and they struck back at the Crane through their advisor. Kunimichi, serving his Kolat handlers, recruited Kogo a second time for this murder.

---

### THE SURVIVOR (For Now)

#### **Ikoma Ujiaki** *(Survived Third Attack)*
**Clan:** Lion (Ikoma Family) | **Role:** Imperial Ambassador
**Public Virtue:** **Yū (Courage)**

**Public Reputation:** Ikoma Ujiaki is famous for his unshakeable courage—not the courage of the battlefield, but the courage to speak difficult truths in hostile courts. He has faced down angry daimyō, delivered unpopular Imperial edicts to furious generals, and never once backed down from confrontation. He advises the Emperor on matters of Lion Clan politics with the confidence of a samurai who fears no challenge.

**The Secret:** Ujiaki sought the field of battle in the courts because he is a **coward in the face of death**. He has never drawn his sword in anger. He avoids any situation where physical violence might occur. His "courage" is performative—the bravery of words, not deeds. He knows the whispers: that he is no true Lion, that his courage is mere posturing.

**The Failed Attack:** When the oni came for him, Ujiaki did not stand and fight like a warrior. He ran. He hid. He screamed for guards. His cowardice—the very thing he's spent his life concealing—*saved his life*. The oni expected a Lion's charge; it got a man who fled into a storage room and barricaded the door.

The oni, wounded by guards who finally responded, escaped west into the Shinomen Forest.

**Current Status:** Alive, shaken, and being hailed as a survivor of supernatural attack. He cannot explain how he survived without admitting his cowardice. He is terrified the oni will return. He is right to be afraid—though the true threat will come from Kunimichi's sabotage, not supernatural attack.

**Scheduled Death:** Early in the campaign, Ujiaki will die in what appears to be an accident. Kunimichi's sabotage—a weakened beam, a poisoned meal, a "tragic fall"—will finish what the oni could not. This will create investigative confusion: Was the "failed" attack actually delayed success? Or is there a second killer?

### THE LIVING FOUR

#### **Bayushi Aramoro**
**Clan:** Scorpion (Bayushi Family) | **Role:** Shoju's Half-Brother
**Public Virtue:** **Chūgi (Duty/Loyalty)**

**Public Reputation:** In a clan renowned for deception and manipulation, Bayushi Aramoro stands out as the exception—a Scorpion whose loyalty to the Emperor is absolute and unquestionable. For thirty years, he has served the Hantei line with such devoted faithfulness that even Scorpion's enemies acknowledge his integrity. He has repeatedly sacrificed Scorpion Clan interests when they conflicted with Imperial interests, earning him enmity within his own clan but unwavering Imperial trust.

He is famous for the "Three Refusals"—three separate occasions where the Scorpion Champion offered him bribes, threats, and blackmail to influence the Emperor, and each time Aramoro reported the attempts directly to the throne. The fact that Champion Shoju is his own half-brother makes this even more remarkable.

**The Secret:** Aramoro's legendary loyalty is a mask for the greatest act of betrayal in recent Imperial history—he intends to kill the Emperor himself.

Twenty years ago, Aramoro discovered that Emperor Hantei XXXVIII ordered the secret execution of Aramoro's sixteen-year-old son for "politically inconvenient" knowledge about Imperial corruption. The boy was guilty only of being in the wrong place at the wrong time. The Emperor had him killed and the death staged as a hunting accident. Aramoro learned the truth through his Scorpion network and has spent two decades planning perfect revenge.

**Current Status:** Aramoro is **not behind the current murders**. When the first advisor died, he was as shocked as anyone. Now he is *investigating*—trying to determine who is responsible, whether they threaten his plans, and whether their chaos might serve his purposes.

He faces a choice: Does he compete with the unknown killer? Let them continue as long as he isn't targeted? Try to recruit them? Or use the chaos as cover for finally moving against the Emperor?

**Arc:** Aramoro begins as an observer. He will eventually discover Nasu Kogo and attempt to recruit her through his own Scorpion agent—not knowing she already serves Crane and Kolat masters. When he finally acts, he will be the threat the investigators stopped looking for.

---

#### **Kitsuki Ma'aya**
**Clan:** Dragon (Kitsuki Family) | **Role:** Investigator Emerita
**Public Virtue:** **Gi (Righteousness/Justice)**

**Public Reputation:** Kitsuki Ma'aya spent forty years as one of the Empire's foremost investigators before her retirement to advisory duties. Her record is considered flawless—case after case solved through the famous Kitsuki method of physical evidence and logical deduction. She famously refused to rule in favor of her own clan in a border dispute, citing the evidence without bias. Her judgments are considered beyond appeal because no one doubts her complete impartiality.

**The Secret:** Ma'aya's famous impartiality is a careful construction. Throughout her career, she has used "evidence" (or the strategic absence thereof) to shine light only on the facts that support the conclusions she wanted. Inconvenient evidence has had a habit of getting lost. Witnesses whose testimony didn't fit her theory were discredited on technicalities.

She didn't *lie*—she simply curated truth. She told herself it was in service of justice, that she was steering toward correct outcomes. But she knows: her "perfect record" is a monument to bias disguised as objectivity.

More recently, she accepted a significant bribe to overlook evidence in a murder investigation. The victim's family still awaits justice that will never come.

**Current Status:** Ma'aya is one of the investigators assigned to the advisor murders—poetic irony given her own compromised principles. She is genuinely trying to solve the case, but her instincts keep leading her toward politically convenient conclusions rather than truth.

---

#### **Asako Tsuki**
**Clan:** Phoenix (Asako Family) | **Role:** Shugenja, Healer
**Public Virtue:** **Jin (Compassion/Benevolence)**

**Public Reputation:** Asako Tsuki is renowned throughout Rokugan as the embodiment of mercy. She has dedicated her life to healing the sick, comforting the dying, and advocating for the powerless. Her aunt, Asako Azunami, is the Master of Water—Tsuki grew up immersed in healing magic and its philosophy. She has walked into plague villages without fear, sat with dying peasants when other samurai wouldn't touch them, and wept genuine tears for every patient she couldn't save.

She established the "Compassion Houses"—small clinics in poor districts where anyone, regardless of caste, could receive treatment. This was controversial—some said it disrupted the Celestial Order—but Tsuki's obvious sincerity and the Emperor's tacit approval silenced critics.

**The Secret:** Tsuki has been performing mercy killings for years. She tells herself it's compassion—ending suffering for those beyond saving. But each death has been her choice, not her patients'. She has become the judge of who deserves to continue suffering and who deserves release. Some of her "peaceful passings" were people who could have recovered. Some were people whose deaths benefited others.

The first death was genuine mercy. The tenth was easier. The twentieth was almost routine. She no longer knows where compassion ends and murder begins.

**Current Status:** Tsuki is investigating the use of what appears to be supernatural power in the murders. Her skills as a shugenja allow her to sense spiritual disturbance. She is getting **dangerously close to discovering Nasu Kogo's involvement**.

**Danger:** If Aramoro's conspiracy decides a fourth murder is necessary to distract investigators or silence threats, Asako Tsuki is the likely target. Kogo fears her—and Aramoro's agent might offer Kogo protection in exchange for eliminating the Phoenix who's getting too close to the truth.

---

#### **Shiba Katsuda**
**Clan:** Phoenix (Shiba Family) | **Role:** Lord of Nikesake
**Public Virtue:** **Meiyo (Honor)**

**Public Reputation:** Shiba Katsuda is celebrated as the greatest living warrior of the Phoenix Clan—a seeming contradiction given Phoenix's scholarly reputation, which makes his martial excellence all the more remarkable. He earned the reputation through a career of legendary victories: the defense of Nishiyama Pass, the Thirty-One Duels, the routing of bandits from Nikesake province. His name is synonymous with martial honor.

**The Secret:** Katsuda is skilled—genuinely skilled. But skill alone didn't make him a legend. Every victory has been helped by unfair advantages concealed from history.

His famous defense of Nishiyama Pass? He had advance warning from a spy. The Thirty-One Duels? At least a dozen opponents were subtly poisoned beforehand—not enough to be obvious, but enough to slow their reflexes. His routing of the Nikesake bandits was possible because he had already bribed their second-in-command to betray them at the crucial moment.

Each victory built upon the last. Each new accolade became a heavier weight. He has become addicted to glory even as he loathes himself for how he earns it. He cannot stop seeking victory because stopping would mean examining what he's done.

The cruel irony: if he had simply been himself—talented but fallible—he would have achieved genuine honor through honest effort. Instead, he built a monument to false glory that now imprisons him.

**Current Status:** Katsuda is one of the remaining advisors and knows he could be targeted. He is privately terrified—not of the oni, but that investigation into the murders might somehow expose his own fraudulent history.

## The Three Conspiracies

Unknown to nearly everyone, **three separate conspiracies** are now operating simultaneously, each pursuing different goals but creating compounding chaos. The key insight is that they emerged *sequentially*, not in parallel:

```
ORIGINAL CONSPIRACY (Crane)
         ↓
    Uses KOGO to kill Satoshi
         ↓
KOLAT DISCOVERY (Satoshi was theirs!)
         ↓
    Uses KOGO (via Kunimichi) to kill Toshiyasu
         ↓
ARAMORO'S OPPORTUNITY (observing, then acting)
         ↓
    Eventually recruits KOGO through his own agent
         ↓
KOGO becomes the nexus of THREE handlers
```

---

### Conspiracy 1: The Crane Distraction

**Origin:** Early 1120

**Background:** The Crane Clan suffered two devastating blows:
1. **Tsume Retsu**, lord of Kyotei Castle in the contested Kintani Valley, was assassinated—a humiliating political failure
2. **A massive tsunami** struck the coastline, devastating holdings and requiring resources for recovery

The Crane had been positioning for aggressive moves against both the Scorpion (in court) and the Lion (on the battlefield). These disasters left them operating from a position of unexpected weakness.

**The Solution:** Create a massive distraction. Kill an Imperial advisor in a spectacular, terrifying way. Throw the capital into paranoid chaos while the Crane recover and prepare for their master stroke—the Toshi Ranbo deal.

**The Method:** **Daidoji Masahiro** (a Crane lord) authorized the operation. **Tsume Kunimichi** (a Crane shinobi and saboteur) was tasked with execution. Kunimichi had previously discovered that **Nasu Kogo**, a Phoenix courtier, was secretly a maho-tsukai. Rather than expose her, he recruited her.

**First Murder:** Kogo summoned an oni to kill **Miya Satoshi**. The death was brutal, supernatural, and terrifying—exactly as planned. The capital erupted in fear and suspicion.

**What the Crane Didn't Know:** Miya Satoshi was a Kolat agent. They had just murdered a member of an ancient conspiracy that doesn't forgive.

---

### Conspiracy 2: Kolat Retaliation

**Origin:** Days after Satoshi's death

**Background:** The Kolat—an ancient conspiracy against the kami and the Celestial Order—had cultivated Miya Satoshi for decades. His position as Imperial Herald gave them unparalleled access to Imperial communications. His death was a catastrophic loss.

When Kolat agents investigated, they traced the killing to Crane involvement. They didn't know the full picture—they didn't know about Kogo or the oni—but they knew the Crane were responsible.

**The Response:** Strike back. Kill a Crane advisor. Send a message.

**The Method:** The Kolat activated **Yuki**, a handler who had connections to Kunimichi. Through channels Kunimichi didn't fully understand, he was instructed to eliminate **Doji Toshiyasu**. Kunimichi recruited Kogo a second time.

**Second Murder:** Kogo's oni tore Toshiyasu apart. A Crane killed for a Crane's actions. The Kolat considered the matter addressed—for now.

**What the Kolat Didn't Know:** The third attack would fail. The oni would escape. And their agent Kunimichi would soon be juggling far more than they realized.

---

### Conspiracy 3: Aramoro's Opportunity

**Origin:** After the second murder

**Background:** Bayushi Aramoro has spent twenty years planning revenge against the Emperor for the secret execution of his son. His plan was patient, methodical, waiting for the perfect moment.

Then two Imperial advisors died in supernatural attacks, and a third survived. The capital descended into paranoia. And Aramoro recognized *opportunity*.

**Phase 1 — Observer:** Aramoro watched. He deployed Scorpion agents to investigate. He tried to identify who was behind the killings—and whether they were friend, foe, or tool.

**Phase 2 — Calculate:** He weighed his options. The chaos was perfect cover. But was the unknown killer a threat to his plans? Would they target him next? Could they be recruited?

**Phase 3 — Actor:** Aramoro's agents eventually discovered Nasu Kogo. They didn't know she already worked for Kunimichi (and through him, the Crane and Kolat). They saw only a useful weapon—a maho-tsukai who had already killed two advisors.

Aramoro's agent approached Kogo with an offer: protection and resources in exchange for her services. From Kogo's perspective, this was salvation—Asako Tsuki was getting too close, and Aramoro's agent promised to shield her from investigation.

**Current Status:** Aramoro is recruiting Kogo without knowing she's already compromised. He's preparing to use the chaos as cover for moving against the Emperor. He's the patient threat that investigators will stop looking for once they "solve" the immediate murders.

---

### The Nexus: Nasu Kogo

**Nasu Kogo** is the connection point between all three conspiracies—though none of her handlers realize the full extent of her entanglements.

**Background:** Kogo is a Phoenix courtier who fell to maho years ago in a desperate attempt to save her family from ruin. She has been walking a path of increasing corruption ever since.

**Her Handlers:**
1. **Tsume Kunimichi** (Crane/Kolat) — Recruited her originally, used her for both murders
2. **Aramoro's Agent** (Scorpion) — Recently approached her, offering protection from investigation
3. (Indirectly) **The Crane lords and Kolat masters** — Who give Kunimichi his orders

**What Kogo Knows:**
- She works for Kunimichi (first for Crane purposes, then for whoever Kunimichi serves—she doesn't know about the Kolat specifically)
- She now works for a Scorpion agent (she doesn't know Aramoro is behind them)
- The oni escaped to Shinomen Forest after the third attack failed
- Asako Tsuki is getting dangerously close to discovering her
- Her corruption is deepening with each summoning

**What Kogo Doesn't Know:**
- Aramoro is the mastermind behind her new Scorpion handler
- The Kolat exists and was behind the second murder order
- That Kunimichi knows about her Scorpion contact (he's suspicious but not certain)

**Kogo's Dilemma:** She's serving three masters, each believing she works primarily for them. She's using the Scorpion connection as protection against investigation. She's terrified of the oni's escape being traced back to her. And her soul is slipping further into darkness with every blood ritual.

---

### The Third Conspirator: Tsume Kunimichi

**Tsume Kunimichi** is a Crane shinobi and saboteur who serves as the operational link between the Crane conspiracy and its execution.

**His Masters:**
- **Daidoji Masahiro** — His Crane lord, who authorized the original distraction operation
- **Yuki** — His Kolat handler, who directed the retaliation against Toshiyasu

**What Kunimichi Knows:**
- Nasu Kogo is a maho-tsukai (he discovered this and recruited her rather than exposing her)
- Kogo is also talking to Scorpion agents (he's noticed, he's worried)
- The Crane conspiracy created the first murder
- His Kolat handlers ordered the second murder (though he may not fully understand Kolat as an organization)
- His next assignment: Kill Ikoma Ujiaki through sabotage, creating a non-supernatural murder to confuse investigators

**What Kunimichi Doesn't Know:**
- Aramoro is the Scorpion mastermind approaching Kogo
- That the oni escaped (Kogo hasn't told him)
- The full scope of what he's part of

**His Role Going Forward:** Kunimichi will commit the **third murder**—Ikoma Ujiaki—using clever sabotage methods that look nothing like the oni killings. This creates investigative confusion: a new method, a new pattern, suggesting either a second killer or an evolution in tactics.

## Investigation Threads

### Thread 1: The Capital Investigation
**Focus:** Who killed the advisors? Court intrigue, political maneuvering, social investigation.

**Key Discoveries:**
- The murders are connected to Crane political interests
- Miya Satoshi had secret correspondence that doesn't add up
- Someone is using supernatural methods
- Kunimichi's sabotage creates a contradictory pattern

**Characters:** Court-focused PCs, magistrates, courtiers

### Thread 2: The Shinomen Hunt
**Focus:** What happened to the oni? Wilderness survival, supernatural horror, tracking.

**Key Discoveries:**
- The oni fled west after the failed attack
- It can be tracked to a summoning point
- Physical evidence links back to Kogo
- The oni itself may be more victim than villain—bound, corrupted, suffering

**Characters:** Scouts, shugenja, wilderness-capable PCs

### Thread 3: The Conspiracy Web
**Focus:** Who benefits? Following money, political analysis, connecting dots.

**Key Discoveries:**
- Crane needed distraction (motive for first murder)
- Kolat exists and lost an agent (motive for second murder)
- Aramoro is too interested in the investigation
- Kogo is connected to multiple handlers

**Characters:** Politically savvy PCs, investigators, information brokers

## The Task Force

With paranoia spreading through the capital and standard investigative channels compromised, the Emperor (or a surviving advisor) decides to bring in outside help.

### Why Outsiders?
- Every Great Clan is suspected
- Internal investigators might be compromised
- The supernatural element requires unusual expertise
- Political neutrality is essential

### Potential Task Force Composition
- **Minor Clan Representatives** — Outside the Great Clan web of obligation
- **Ronin** — No clan loyalty to compromise investigation
- **Brotherhood Monks** — Spiritual expertise, political neutrality
- **Imperial Functionaries** — Loyal to throne, not clans
- **Specialized Experts** — Monster hunters, maho investigators

### Troupe Play Structure
Players control multiple characters across different threads:
- **Court Character** — Navigates the political investigation
- **Wilderness Character** — Tracks the oni to Shinomen
- **Specialist Character** — Brings unique expertise (shugenja, investigator, etc.)

Characters can die; players continue with remaining characters. Information is shared between sessions through "downtime reports."
