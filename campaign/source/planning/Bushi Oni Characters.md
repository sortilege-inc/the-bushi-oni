Hiruma Kaede is a powerfully built young woman whose weathered features and thousand-yard stare belie her mere twenty years. Broad-shouldered and muscular from relentless training and Wall defense, she moves with the deliberate economy of a veteran warrior. A thin scar runs from her left temple to her ear, while her calloused hands and sun-darkened skin speak to countless hours exposed to harsh elements. Her most distinctive feature is her deliberately mismatched armor—a breastplate from a fallen comrade, arm guards inherited from her instructor, leg protection she modified herself—all worn beneath a weather-beaten purple Unicorn traveling cloak that stands out starkly against traditional Crab colors. Dark hair tied back with plain cord, strong angular features set in serious neutrality, and dark eyes that observe more than they reveal complete the picture of a dutiful soldier—except for those rare moments when her gaze drifts to distant horizons, and something hungry and alive flickers across her face before discipline reasserts itself.
