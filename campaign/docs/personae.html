Who the task force has met, or heard of

Dramatis Personae

As they stand after Session Three. What is written here is what the three of them know.

The task force

Kitsuki Hasumi

Dragon · Kitsuki investigator · sent by Kitsuki Jusai

A Kitsuki investigator trained at Last Step Castle, lately in the defences the Kitsuki teach against shinobi. She carries a jian, keeps her hands gloved and leaves the burn scar on her face uncovered. She has a precise memory and uses it, and in her experience the kami and oni are blamed for far more than they do. She is in contact with Seppun Hokuto, on her lord's instructions and without Satsume's knowledge.

Isawa Endo

Phoenix · Isawa shugenja · sent by Isawa Uona

An Isawa shugenja whose masters think well of his way with the elements, fire and earth in particular. He wears gold-framed glasses and red robes and likes to be seen. He trusts the kami absolutely; his divination of Ikoma Ujiaki's journey home was overtaken by another fate, and it shook him.

Bayushi Taigen

Scorpion · son of the Clan Champion · trained with the Hida

A son of Bayushi Shoju and Bayushi Kachiko, and twin to Bayushi Dairu. His father sent him to the Crab lands, where he served on the Carpenter Wall and trained as a Hida Defender. He wears black, heavier armour than a Scorpion usually does, two katana and an ōtsuchi. He is betrothed to Doji Shizue.

Isawa Renji

Phoenix · a boy of Endo's school

A boy of twelve or thirteen who has not yet come of age. He is of Endo's school, though not his pupil, and Endo brought him to the capital to see something of the Empire. He asked Hasumi, innocently, why she carries only one sword. Endo told him to watch his mouth.

Those who sent them

Doji Satsume

Crane · the Emerald Champion

The Emerald Champion, who set the task force up to run beside his magistrates' investigation and apart from it. He works in the Crane district, finishes what he is writing before he looks up, and recites facts in order as he paces. He lent them the use of his seal, then left the capital to follow a lead of his own, promising to be back within two weeks.

龍

Kitsuki Jusai

Dragon · Hasumi's lord

Hasumi's lord, at Last Step Castle. Her own sheet says he leads a clandestine network, the Eyes and Ears of the Dragon. He almost never gives private audiences; he gave her one, a sealed letter for Seppun Hokuto, and her place on the task force.

鳳

Isawa Uona

Phoenix · Endo's lord

Endo's lord and master. Uona sent him into the forest shrines to work on his invocations and his humility, then recalled him with Satsume's request for someone who could examine spiritual disturbances.

Bayushi Kachiko

Scorpion · Taigen's mother

Taigen's mother, with whom he is on good terms. She persuaded his father to send Taigen in answer to Satsume's summons and brought it to him at Kyūden Doji, together with the family's own intelligence on the dead. She agreed to write to the Crab about similar attacks.

Bayushi Shoju

Scorpion · Clan Champion · Taigen's father

Champion of the Scorpion Clan and Taigen's father. Satsume's summons went first to him. Taigen does not expect much affection from him; it was Shoju who sent him to the Crab lands.

Close to Taigen

Doji Shizue

Crane · Taigen's betrothed

Taigen's betrothed, from Kyūden Doji, who keeps rooms in the capital. She travelled north with him and wrote his first, unsuccessful request for an audience with Ikoma Ujiaki.

Bayushi Dairu

Scorpion · Taigen's twin

Taigen's twin and the elder of the two. They would be inseparable if their father did not keep sending them different ways.

Bayushi Aramoro

Scorpion · Taigen's uncle

Taigen's uncle, his father's brother, who wears a mask. He was pleasantly surprised to find his nephew in the capital, read Satsume's writ when Taigen showed it to him discreetly, and offered his help. Taigen wrote to him when the tunnel was found, and he came down into the cellar himself, named the disguises "infiltrators", and brought the news of Ujiaki's death.

The dead

Miya Satoshi

Imperial · the Imperial Herald · first victim

Imperial Herald for more than twenty years, famously honest, with a flawless memory and the Emperor's close confidence. About three months ago he was found in his private chambers in the Forbidden City torn into three pieces. The Scorpion have reason, but no proof, to think his loyalties to the Empire were mixed. His rooms have been cleaned; beneath a rug in them is a hatch to a cellar, and beyond the cellar the tunnels.

Doji Toshiyasu

Crane · Imperial delegate · second victim

An Imperial delegate known for tea ceremonies at which he brought hostile parties together. He was killed in the Forbidden City within two weeks of the task force's arrival, torn in half at the waist. He was found with his wakizashi in his hand; two shallow cuts on him are, by Hasumi's reading, his own blade turned back against him. There were growths clustered at the base of his spine, and every wound had bled while he lived. His body is to go home to his family in the Crane lands.

Ikoma Ujiaki

Lion · Imperial ambassador · attacked, then dead

An Imperial ambassador who spent much of his time in Lion lands, loud and hospitable, with strong opinions about sake. He survived the third attack and called it an illusion and a prank, and he was lying. His finger of jade was worn to a sliver and his left arm had grown several inches too long. He left the capital for home, and Bayushi Aramoro reports that a bridge on the outskirts collapsed under his party and that he and his men died.

In the capital

Seppun Hokuto

Imperial · Seppun · Jusai's correspondent

An investigator Jusai calls an exemplar of the Kitsuki's methods, though he is a Seppun. His office is several rooms opened into one, full of tables, with diagrams and maps where art would hang. He took Jusai's letter from Hasumi and told her not to mention him or it to Satsume, and to send word once she had been briefed.

Kitsuki Kagi

Dragon · Emerald magistrate · Satsume's yoriki

Satsume's yoriki, who runs the first investigation into the killings. The task force is not to interfere with it or share anything with it. His people went through all three rooms before the task force arrived.

鶴

Daidoji Masahiro

Crane · come to take Toshiyasu home

A man in his early sixties, dressed in cream, who watched the examination of Toshiyasu's body without expression and without objection. He says he is not usually in the capital and came to see the body home. Endo once overheard a Daidoji Masahiro named as the man behind the Daidoji Harriers, the Crane's shinobi, who officially do not exist.

Tsume Rin

Crane · Toshiyasu's yojimbo

Toshiyasu's yojimbo. Her written testimony says she heard a struggle, went in, and found him dead and the killer gone. She kept a still face over his body, but flinched when Endo said she had not been able to protect him. She confirmed that his wakizashi had been bloodied before it was cleaned.

獅

Ikoma Ujiaki's yojimbo

Lion · name not given

A very large man in Lion colours with touches of the Mirumoto in his dress. He stood guard in silence throughout the task force's audience with Ujiaki. By Ujiaki's account, he was the one who came in after the attack.

Ide Mihoko

Unicorn · Ide courtier

A Unicorn courtier met at a teahouse. In the week before his death she took tea with Doji Toshiyasu and Asako Kikue, whom she would rather not have met, over a dispute that was not settled. She had heard that his last tea ceremony was with the brother of the Scorpion Clan Champion. Endo sensed a fire kami bound in the medallion she wears.

Asako Kikue

Phoenix · inquisitor · not yet met

A Phoenix inquisitor. Endo remembers her name as someone who served with the Crab as a field healer and spiritual intervener and knows the Taint first-hand. Ide Mihoko met her at Toshiyasu's tea, where the two of them did not get along. Whether she is still in the capital is not known.
