Legend of the Five Rings · Otosan Uchi · early 1120

The Bushi Oni

Three people close to the Emperor have been attacked inside the Imperial capital, and two of them are dead. Miya Satoshi, the Imperial Herald, was found in his private chambers in the Forbidden City torn into three pieces. Within a few weeks Doji Toshiyasu, an Imperial delegate of the Crane, was found in the Forbidden City torn in half at the waist. The Lion ambassador Ikoma Ujiaki was attacked in his quarters and survived.

The rooms were closed, some of them locked and some with a bodyguard at the door. Nobody saw anything come or go. The bodies look as though a great beast mauled them.

The Emerald Champion, Doji Satsume, already has an investigation under way: his yoriki (sworn deputy), the Emerald magistrate Kitsuki Kagi, runs it from within the capital. Satsume has now started a second one, running beside it and kept wholly apart from it. For it he wrote to three lords across the Empire and asked each for someone outside the magistracy. Each sent one person, and none of the three had met the others before they sat down in Satsume's waiting room.

The task force

Kitsuki HasumiDragon · Kitsuki Investigator
Isawa EndoPhoenix · Isawa Elementalist
Bayushi TaigenScorpion · Hida Defender

Kitsuki Hasumi was sent by her lord Kitsuki Jusai from Last Step Castle, where she had been training in the defences a Kitsuki investigator needs against shinobi. She fights with a jian, a straight sword from beyond the Empire's borders that an opponent is unlikely to have faced. She leaves a burn scar on her face uncovered.

Isawa Endo is an Isawa shugenja whose masters think well of his way with the elements, fire and earth in particular; his lord Isawa Uona put his name forward when Satsume asked the Phoenix for someone who could examine spiritual disturbances. He wears gold-framed glasses and traditional red robes, and he brought the boy Isawa Renji from Phoenix lands to see something of the Empire.

Bayushi Taigen is a son of the Scorpion Clan Champion. He served on the Carpenter Wall with the Crab and trained with the Hida, and he carries two katana, a wakizashi and an ōtsuchi (a great war hammer). His mother, Bayushi Kachiko, brought him the Emerald Champion's summons at Kyūden Doji, where he was visiting his betrothed.
